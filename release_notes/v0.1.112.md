# Notepatra v0.1.112

**Noter — the system-of-record wave.** A 19-agent usability audit graded Noter **C-**: great for live-meeting scratch capture, not trustworthy as a meeting system of record — reminders silently never fired, search couldn't find note bodies, save failures were invisible, and an unreachable AI backend froze the app. This release fixes every CRITICAL/HIGH finding properly (no quick-win patches), plus a 3-layer hardening of the coding assistant's `apply_diff` loop. Same bare binary, no new dependencies.

## What changed

### Your reminders fire now
- The reminder engine used to live inside the Noter tab — close the tab (or never open it) and reminders silently died, while Help promised desktop notifications. The engine is now **app-lifetime**: reminders fire whenever Notepatra is running, Noter tab or not.
- Reminders that came due while Notepatra was closed arrive as **one catch-up digest** on the next launch instead of vanishing.
- Honest wording everywhere: Help, the menu status tip and the Welcome card now say exactly when reminders fire.

### Your notes are safe
- **Save failures are loud**: a red **NOT SAVED** hint + a banner with **Save a copy as…** — no more stderr-only failure under a footer claiming "auto-saves in 5s". A failed read no longer opens a blank editor that autosave then writes over your real file.
- **External edits can't be clobbered**: if a note changed on disk under Noter, the conflicting save becomes a **conflict copy** beside the original.
- Saves are **fsync-durable**, and a `.draft` sidecar bounds crash loss to ≤2 seconds of typing.
- Clicking checklist **text** places the caret; only the checkbox itself toggles done-state.

### You can find things again
- **Search reads note bodies**, not just titles — AND-of-terms, auto-expanding groups, match counts, snippet tooltips. New notes titled "Noter NN" are no longer unfindable.
- **One title everywhere**: sidebar, tab, popout and body H1 can no longer disagree.
- Headers survive save+reload; rename keeps the date prefix; the 99-note sidebar cap is gone.

### Extract behaves
- Ollama down? Extract tells you in about a second instead of freezing the app behind a wait cursor. Pre-flight check, 125 s watchdog, visible **Cancel**.
- Extract results (Decisions / Questions / Risks) now **persist into the note**, and re-running **replaces** the old run instead of stacking duplicates.

### Chrome & polish
- Popout works more than once (was permanently dead after first close).
- **Noter follows Dark / Monokai themes** (was hardcoded light-cream).
- All 7 Noter shortcuts documented in Help; toolbar chips have tooltips.

### AI coding assistant — `apply_diff` hardening
- Malformed patches (escaped-newline payloads, degenerate hunks) are rejected tool-side with a corrective message before touching your file.
- The tool contract gives the model a recovery ladder after a failed patch (re-read → full-content fallback) — phrased model-agnostically.
- A perseveration breaker stops the agent from retrying the identical failing call forever; it finalizes with an honest summary instead.

## Notes

- All Noter fixes are subsystem-local except the authorized app-level reminder-service hooks.
- No regressions: **full ctest green (Lite + Full)**, including two new suites (`test_notes_extract_apply`, `test_noter_render`) and 60+ widget-harness sections; built and hand-verified across Light / Dark / Monokai.
- Existing notes upgrade in place: first launch may show a one-time catch-up digest for reminders that were silently missed by older versions; sidebar groups now bucket by creation date.
