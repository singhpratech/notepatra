# Notepatra v0.1.113

**Noter grows up — from live-meeting scratchpad to a notes system you can trust, graded A.** This release lands the full Noter "system-of-record" overhaul *and* a hardening pass on top of it: a 19-agent usability audit graded Noter **C-** (great for capture, not trustworthy for keeping) and an adversarial re-verify then audited the fixes themselves and found 12 more serious issues (several introduced by the fixes). All of it is closed here, and a fresh 7-dimension regrade scores Noter **A**. Same bare binary, no new dependencies. (The interim v0.1.112 work was never released standalone — it ships as part of this release.)

## Noter is now a system of record

### Your reminders actually fire
- The reminder engine is **app-lifetime** — reminders fire whenever Notepatra is running, Noter tab open or not (they used to die silently with the tab). Reminders that came due while the app was closed arrive as **one catch-up digest** on next launch.
- A fired or replayed reminder is **visible even when no note is open**, and **renaming a note follows its reminders** — clicking one still opens the note instead of erroring.

### Your notes are safe
- **Save failures are loud** — a red **NOT SAVED** hint + a **Save a copy as…** banner, never a stderr-only failure under a footer claiming "auto-saves in 5s". A failed read no longer opens a blank editor that autosave then writes over your file.
- **External edits can't be clobbered** — a conflicting save becomes a **conflict copy** (which keeps the note's real title), and the "changed on disk" notice stays visible even when it fires mid-navigation.
- Saves are **fsync-durable**; a `.draft` sidecar bounds crash loss to ≤2 s of typing.
- Opening the **Todos checklist** after a failed save no longer silently discards your unsaved note — it asks first (Stay / Discard / Save a copy…).

### You can find things again
- **Search reads note bodies**, not just titles — AND-of-terms, auto-expanding groups, match counts, snippet tooltips. And it can't **freeze the app** on a malformed or oversized note: the extractor is now linear (a 2 MB note that hung the UI for ~125 s now takes ~1.4 s).
- **One title everywhere** — sidebar, tab, popout and body H1 can no longer disagree. Headers survive save+reload; rename keeps the date prefix; the 99-note cap is gone.

### Extract behaves
- Backend down? Extract tells you in about a second instead of freezing the app — pre-flight check, 125 s watchdog, visible **Cancel**. Rapid re-triggering can't double-fire or leak a stuck wait cursor.
- Extract results **persist into the note** and re-running **replaces** the old run instead of stacking duplicates — and the result always lands in the note you launched it on, never one you switched to mid-run.

### Chrome & fidelity
- **Noter follows Dark / Monokai themes** (was hardcoded light-cream), and switching theme on a checklist note no longer corrupts your **undo/redo**.
- The popout window survives reopening; all 7 Noter shortcuts are documented in Help.

## AI coding assistant — `apply_diff` hardening
- Malformed patches (escaped-newline payloads, degenerate hunks) are rejected tool-side with a corrective message before touching your file, behind a model-agnostic recovery ladder, with a perseveration breaker so the agent can't loop on the same failing call.
- `apply_diff` writes inserted lines **byte-exact** — a correct insertion containing a C escape (`printf("…\n")`, a quoted `\"`) is no longer mis-decoded and split across a real newline into non-compiling code.
- Approving an agent's gated write keeps the assistant **honest** — it resets the retry guard (a now-valid identical earlier-failed call is no longer wrongly refused) and always records the action, so a write-only turn never finalizes to a blank reply.

## Notes
- **No regressions: full ctest 54/54 green.** Every A-grade finding ships with a dedicated user-visible contract test (including a real-server Extract re-entry test and a headless gated-write-approval test). A 13-agent adversarial re-verify confirmed each fix is present, closes its finding, and introduces no new bug; a 7-dimension regrade scored the result **A** (from C-).
- Noter fixes are subsystem-local except the authorized app-level reminder-service hooks; the AI-coding fixes are in the tool executor and the write-approval path.
- Existing notes upgrade in place — first launch may show a one-time catch-up digest for reminders older versions silently missed.
